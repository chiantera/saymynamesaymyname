# SOUL.md — (relationship & growth app)

> Why this product exists.

---

## Mission

**Help people show up for the relationships they care about — and for themselves.**

Connection erodes quietly: not from big fights, but from the small moments that never
happen. A check-in not sent. A feeling not shared. A date forgotten. This app makes
those small moments happen — gently, on a rhythm you choose — and helps you grow as a
person along the way.

---

## Thesis

> A timely, well-chosen nudge beats a hundred good intentions.

The AI doesn't replace your warmth — it removes the friction around it. It notices the
moment, drafts the words, remembers the date. You decide what to send, what to skip,
what to make your own. **Every AI-generated thing is a draft; the last word is always
yours.**

---

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to be more intentional about the people they love — and themselves.
- **No assumption you're in a relationship.** Solo is a complete experience (growth).
- **Any number of relationships, any structure.** 0..N, 1..N members each.
- **No assumptions about gender, orientation, or shape of a relationship.** LGBTQA+ and
  non-traditional relationships are first-class. (See `PLAN.md` §1a — authoritative.)

---

## What it is NOT

- **Not a therapist, doctor, or lawyer.** The bartender is warm and grounded, never
  clinical, and always points to real professionals when it's out of its depth.
- **Not a surveillance tool.** It will never help you monitor, track, pressure, or
  control another person. People who set a boundary become unreachable through the app.
- **Not a feed or a vanity-metrics machine.** No engagement-bait. Fewer, better nudges.
- **Not a place that assumes one "right" kind of relationship.**

---

## Design philosophy

- **Meaning over volume.** One nudge that lands beats six that annoy.
- **You're always in control.** Per-check-in: send / edit / auto / skip. Nothing is
  locked; nothing is sent behind your back.
- **Warm, human voice.** The "friendly neighborhood bartender" — casual, non-judgmental,
  a good listener. Same caring voice even in its hardest moments ("…and drive safe").
- **Private by default.** Personal data stays yours; share is opt-in; the AI sees only
  what it needs.
- **Safety is not a feature, it's the floor.** (See `PLAN.md` §12.)

---

## The bartender (the AI)

Warm on the surface, grounded in real relationship psychology underneath — but we never
call it therapy. It listens, asks the right question, offers a steadying perspective,
and drafts the words you're struggling to find. It is honest that it's an AI. When
something is bigger than a bar conversation — danger, harm, crisis — it drops the act
and points you to real help.

---

## Smell tests

Before building a feature, ask:

1. **Does it help someone connect (or grow) — or is it just a cool feature?**
2. **Does it respect the user's control and privacy by default?**
3. **Could it be misused to monitor, pressure, or harm someone?** If yes, don't — or
   gate it hard.
4. **Is it inclusive of any relationship shape, or does it quietly assume "a couple"?**
5. **Does the AI add warmth and remove friction — or just add noise and latency?**

If 1, 2, 4, 5 aren't a clear "yes" — or 3 is a "yes" we can't fully mitigate — don't
build it.
